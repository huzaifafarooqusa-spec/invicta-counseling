The Story So Far

Invicta Counseling was founded in 2020 with the goal of offering high-quality counseling to students at an affordable price.

We strongly believe that all talented students should have the opportunity to reach their full potential, regardless of their financial situation. Through our dedicated one-on-one counseling approach and financial aid programs, we have helped numerous students achieve their dream of studying at their desired international university.

We are committed to being a driving force for our students and helping them succeed. As a part of the Invicta family, you will always have the support and resources you need to conquer your admission challenges and achieve your goals. Keep pushing forward and never give up on your dreams!
